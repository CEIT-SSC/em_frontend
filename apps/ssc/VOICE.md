# SSC Persian voice

The approved homepage hero is the reference for all SSC interface copy, from public pages to forms, dashboard messages, and accessible labels.

> از هم یاد می‌گیریم، با هم می‌سازیم
>
> ما انجمن علمی مهندسی کامپیوتر امیرکبیریم. کنار هم یاد می‌گیریم و می‌سازیم؛ از دنیای متن‌باز و بازی‌سازی تا هوش مصنوعی و رقابت‌های برنامه‌نویسی.

## Sound like the students running the association

Write directly in Persian. Think about what we would naturally say to another student; do not draft English marketing copy and translate it. Use a warm, contemporary voice with clear sentences and concrete activities. Prefer active first-person plural when the association speaks: «انجمن رو خودمون می‌چرخونیم»، «تاریخش رو اعلام می‌کنیم».

Keep the hero as the approved reference, not a grammar template to copy mechanically. In new conversational prose, use a consistent spoken Persian register: «خودمون»، «دلمون می‌خواد»، «می‌شه»، «رو» and «بهمون پیام بدید». Never mix a conversational introduction such as «امیرکبیریم» with stiff continuations such as «کارهای انجمن دست خودمان است». Rewrite the whole sentence as a student would say it. Keep conventional task labels such as «ثبت‌نام» and «ذخیره تغییرات» short and recognizable. Avoid switching to singular address, heavy slang, forced memes, emoji, exaggerated enthusiasm, or bureaucratic phrases such as «اقدام فرمایید» and «جهت بهره‌مندی».

Each section should say something useful of its own. Reserve the hero slogan for the hero; do not repeat it or use variations of it as every heading. Explain our purpose through learning, trying ideas, finding teammates, and organizing events rather than abstract claims about innovation or excellence.

## Match the moment

- Public sections can feel inviting and curious: «بعدش چه خبر؟».
- Buttons name the action: «برای همکاری پیام بدید»، «ذخیره تغییرات».
- Forms give short, practical instructions: «یک آدرس ایمیل معتبر وارد کنید» (task instructions can use familiar standard forms; surrounding prose should stay conversational).
- Errors explain what failed and what to do next: «اطلاعات ذخیره نشد. دوباره تلاش کنید.»
- Success messages confirm the result briefly: «تیم ساخته شد».
- Payments, account security, and deletion stay calm and precise. Keep consequences and recovery information; avoid jokes or invented guarantees.
- Empty states distinguish an empty list from a loading failure. Never imply there are no events just because their request failed.

## Spelling and terminology

Use plain «ه» for words such as «درباره»، «دوره» and «صفحه». Never append U+0654 COMBINING HAMZA ABOVE to heh, and never substitute U+06C0 ARABIC LETTER HEH WITH YEH ABOVE. This applies to visible copy, metadata, accessible names, and new writing examples. Keep legitimate hamza in words such as «تأیید» and «مسئله».

Use Persian ی and ک, natural half-spaces, and Persian numerals in authored Persian prose. Keep URLs, code, dates used by machines, and official event names in their required format.

Names, official roles, generation numbers, event dates, contact destinations, and backend content remain factual. Rewrite the surrounding explanation without inventing program details, dates, sponsor benefits, or product behavior. The sponsor contact is @ceitssc; the public news channel is @ceit_ssc.

## Before shipping copy

Read each complete interaction aloud, including dynamic values and adjacent labels. Check that the instruction matches the actual validation or action, repeated concepts use the same terms, and longer messages fit on phones. Scan changed authored text for the prohibited Unicode forms above. Preserve the approved hero title and description unless the user explicitly requests a change.
