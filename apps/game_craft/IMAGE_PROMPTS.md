# Homepage Artwork Production Briefs

These are production briefs for the graphic team, not HTML alt text. No generated or licensed placeholder imagery is used in the application.

## GC-ART-01 - `deep-atrium-hero`

- **Placement:** Homepage introduction, behind the event title and primary calls to action.
- **Scene:** A cut-away university maker hall below ground with vaulted ribs, suspended drafting lights, distant desks, and two tiny anonymous student silhouettes working. Use deep foreground columns, misted middle depth, and a pale-blue light well.
- **Composition:** Keep the central 50% low-detail for localized copy. Do not include text, logos, game UI, recognizable masks, character silhouettes, horns, or protected imagery.
- **Delivery:** 2880x1620 desktop (16:9) and 1440x1920 mobile (3:4). Mobile preserves the upper light well and lower silhouettes.
- **Accessible description:** Abstract blue-lit vaulted workshop behind the GameCraft introduction.

## GC-ART-02 - `processional-map`

- **Placement:** Homepage transition between introduction and event timeline.
- **Scene:** A long procession of original subterranean arches and bridges joined by an etched route with seven abstract location markers and pale lanterns.
- **Composition:** Keep the route readable at center and both edges crop-safe. It must not resemble a recognizable game map or use text.
- **Delivery:** 2880x960 desktop (3:1) and 1440x1800 mobile (4:5).
- **Accessible description:** An abstract illuminated route marking the GameCraft event journey.

## GC-ART-03 - `forge-threshold`

- **Placement:** Homepage entrance to the Game Jam and competition section.
- **Scene:** A contemporary student maker threshold: one broad stone-and-metal arch, pinned sketches, cable-like geometry, cool blue lamps, restrained violet shadow, and distant human-scale silhouettes.
- **Composition:** Preserve a clear logical-start text zone that can mirror for RTL. Avoid combat, weapons, fantasy lore, logos, characters, and text.
- **Delivery:** 2880x1620 desktop (16:9) and 1440x1920 mobile (3:4).
- **Accessible description:** An abstract illuminated gateway marking the GameCraft competition.

## GC-ART-04 - `study-grotto`

- **Placement:** Homepage transition from competitions to workshops and presentations.
- **Scene:** A calm learning chamber with tiered benches, suspended notes, sparse fungal shelves, a small amount of vine growth, and cool blue light filtering through stone.
- **Composition:** Reserve the upper third for a section heading. Moss is a minor environmental detail only.
- **Delivery:** 2400x1200 desktop (2:1) and 1440x1800 mobile (4:5).
- **Accessible description:** An abstract blue-lit learning chamber for GameCraft workshops.

## GC-ART-05 - `collegium-lightwell`

- **Placement:** Homepage About section beside the association story and team call to action.
- **Scene:** A communal university lightwell with abstract makers arranging a circular project table, distant architectural ruins, delicate etched linework, and pale floating light.
- **Composition:** Human figures remain ordinary, faceless student silhouettes. Keep a central open field for heading and body copy.
- **Delivery:** 2400x1350 desktop (16:9) and 1440x1800 mobile (4:5).
- **Accessible description:** An abstract gathering of student makers beneath a blue-lit atrium.

## GC-ART-07 - `gallery-lightwell`

- **Placement:** Gallery route header before the supplied event photographs.
- **Scene:** A quiet blue-lit photographic lightwell with blank archival frames, distant vault ribs, and a small amount of floating pale light.
- **Composition:** The middle 60% stays clear for the page title; the scene introduces the photo archive without competing with real images. Avoid text, logos, game UI, characters, or recognizable game architecture.
- **Delivery:** 2400x900 desktop (8:3) and 1440x1440 mobile (1:1).
- **Accessible description:** An abstract vaulted lightwell preparing the visitor for GameCraft event photographs.
