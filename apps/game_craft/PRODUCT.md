# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Students and early-career game developers who want to learn, meet collaborators, join a team, and take part in a university game-development event.

## Product Purpose

GameCraft is Amirkabir University of Technology's fifth game-development event. It brings workshops, presentations, networking, team formation, a game jam, and a closing recognition ceremony into one participant journey.

## Positioning

The event connects university learning and industry knowledge to a practical team game-development competition.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The bilingual Persian/English web app presents the public event programme, workshop and competition enrolment, sponsorship, staff, and participant dashboard flows. Preserve localized content, real event imagery, sponsor logos, posters, authentication, and purchasing behavior.

## Brand Commitments

The approved homepage direction is an original subterranean wayfinder atelier: architectural, luminous, and modern, without copied Hollow Knight characters, logos, maps, screenshots, or game assets. The public experience may be atmospheric, but interactive content must remain clear and accessible.

## Evidence on Hand

Event content and translations are in `messages/`; supplied staff, sponsor, gallery, and poster assets are in `public/`. New environmental artwork is not yet available and must use documented, stable placeholders until the graphic team supplies it.

## Product Principles

- Help visitors understand the event and find the next action quickly.
- Make learning, collaboration, and making feel connected.
- Treat real programme information and participant tasks as more important than decoration.
- Keep the experience accessible in both supported directions and on mobile.
